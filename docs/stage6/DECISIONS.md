# Stage 6 Decisions

This is the durable decision log for the Stage 6 Presentation Factory. Evidence
labels use `CONFIRMED_SOURCE`, `CONFIRMED_RUNTIME`, `INFERRED`, or `PLANNED`.

## 2026-08-12 — Human-gate policy

- Evidence: explicit Stage 6 master authorization (`CONFIRMED_SOURCE`).
- Stage 6A selects its visual direction autonomously. Aesthetic choice among
  viable directions is not an interruption condition.
- Stage 6L is the only planned creative human gate, after integrated automation
  and AI visual review have produced a coherent review candidate.
- Earlier interruption is limited to an external-access, legal/license, or
  destructive irreversible high-risk decision that cannot be resolved safely.

## 2026-08-12 — Authoring boundary

- Canonical presentation intent belongs in diffable project-owned sources.
- Deterministic compilers/adapters own Nitro formats, allocations, and binary
  serialization.
- Runtime QA combines semantic state with native-resolution screenshots.
- A one-off patch is evidence only when it also establishes a reusable authoring
  boundary needed by Stage 7.
